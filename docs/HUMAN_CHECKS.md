## Personal case study and contribution inventory

Write in your own words:

1. What study problem did you actually observe, and when?
2. What did your first attempt do? Attach a dated original sketch or screenshot you own.
3. Explain three decisions: local Canvas input, revision conflict handling, and evidence-linked review records. Which decisions did you make, and what did AI help implement?
4. Describe one failure you personally reproduced and how you verified its fix.
5. List specific files/features you designed, wrote, debugged, tested or learned. Mark AI-generated portions explicitly.
6. What remains uncertain after the physical tests? What would you change next, based on evidence?

Contribution table: **date | feature/file | my action | AI/other help | original evidence | what I can explain**. Leave unknowns blank until confirmed. Tests do not prove interview understanding.

Interview practice: trace a completed stroke to its disk revision; explain lost HTTP response versus failed disk write; show why a correction appends history instead of replacing a review. Make one bounded improvement from CONTRIBUTING.md and explain the test yourself if you choose to do it.
